Your wedding band sets the tone for a huge part of your day, so it's worth taking the time to get the choice right. Here's what we'd suggest looking for.

## Versatility over volume

Not all wedding bands are able to play chilled tunes through your drinks reception and then bring the energy for your evening party — But The Upbeats Duo are. If you want to get two separate acts to play at different times throughout the day, that's totally fine. The advantage of booking The Upbeats Duo is that there's less to worry about, they can play the conversation-filled cocktail hour and then bring the party in the evening. Leaving you to focus on enjoying your special day.

## A track record you can check

Look for reviews from real couples, and don't be shy about asking for references or recent wedding dates you could ask about directly.

## Clear, easy communication

From your first enquiry through to the final song of the night, you want a band that's easy to reach, quick to respond, and happy to talk through the details — first dance song, timings, any special requests. Some bands are just thrown together and have hardly rehearsed, let alone gigged together. And musicians aren't always the most reliable of people. So find a band that you can trust.

## Local knowledge

A band that's played extensively across the North West will already know many of the region's most popular wedding venues, which can make planning on the day much smoother.
