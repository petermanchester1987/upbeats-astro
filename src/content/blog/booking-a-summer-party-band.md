Summer is peak season for live music — weddings, garden parties, festivals and corporate away-days all compete for the same weekends, which means good bands get booked up fast.

## Book early

If you've got your eye on a date between May and September, the earlier you enquire the better. Popular Saturdays can be booked out six to twelve months in advance, especially for wedding season.

## Think about your setlist

A good party band will take a few of your requests ahead of time and build a set around your crowd. Let your band know if there are songs you definitely want played — and just as importantly, anything you'd rather they skipped. There's nothing worse than hearing the first few bars of your kryptonite song when you've just gotten on the dancefloor! 

## Ask about logistics

For outdoor summer events, ask your band what they need in terms of power, shelter and a backup plan for weather. Most bands won't have a plan for wet weather, so it needs to be discussed well in advance... especially if your event is in the UK.

## Check equipment and insurance

A professional act should bring their own PA system and lighting, and carry public liability insurance. Don't be afraid to ask for proof — any reputable band will have it ready to send over.
